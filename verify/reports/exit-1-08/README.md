## 1. What a process exercises

Every process is a program over seeded inputs — decimals of random scale and sign, integers,
dates, money in currencies of 0, 2 and 3 minor units, texts with non-ASCII letters,
ligatures, combining marks, Greek and CJK, lists — through the determinism-sensitive
functions of STD-EXPR-005: exact decimal arithmetic, `div` and `round` with rounding modes,
`floor`, `ceil`, `clamp`; money `amount`, `allocate`, `format`; `add_days`, `add_months`
(end-of-month rule), `days_between`, `weekday`, `end_of_month`, `to_zone` into seven zones
including half-hour and 45-minute offsets; `upper`, `lower` (full Unicode case mapping, NFC),
`trim`, `substr`, `concat`, `replace`, `pad_left`, `contains`, `format`; `sum`, `sort_by`,
`map`, `filter`, `max`, `min`, `count`, `distinct`, `any`, `all`; `sys.uuid` from the seeded
entropy and `ctx.now` from the fixed clock; `if` and `repeat` statements. L2 scripts (Rhai)
exercise `dec` arithmetic and comparison, string case mapping, length, concatenation,
sorting and `sys.uuid`.

The outcome of a process is the canonical JSON of its result (or error code), the metering
units and the instruction counts; its hash is compared with the recorded one. Every tenth
process is also replayed from its journal (`replay`), which must report `identical`.

## 2. Reproduce

```sh
cargo build --release --locked -p ubos-dvm --bin ubos-replay
./target/release/ubos-replay check --expected verify/replay/expected.json --report replay-$(uname -s)-$(uname -m).json
```

CI runs the check on Linux x86-64 on every push; the nightly workflow on the three platforms.
