# Step 9 record — finance Buk, part 1 (PH-2 step 9e, DEC-145)

The record of PH-2 step 9 (DEC-141…145): the tie-out, the inspection for EXIT-2-04
(VER-INSP-0002) and how far step 9 covers the general-ledger, period and reporting requirements
of `docs/finance-requirements` (fin-req-v1.0).

## 1. Tie-out

`docs/tie-out.md` (generated by `tools/tieout_report.py`): 12 tests compare 466 figures of
FIN-EXP-01…07 and FIN-EXP-15…17 with the Buk's results; all pass in memory and on PostgreSQL 16.
The general-ledger part of FIN-EXP-17 is tied out; the open balance of customer C400 belongs
to receivables (step 10). FIN-EXP-08…14 belong to step 10.

## 2. Inspection for EXIT-2-04 (VER-INSP-0002)

**Procedure 1 — identifier scan.** `docs/tools/check_kernel_purity.py` scans the kernel crates
for finance identifiers outside tests, test drivers and examples, and classifies every hit by an
explicit rule. Result on 2026-11-01: 172 hits, all classified, **0 findings**. The hits are the
execution journal (STD-CTX-011), the chart-of-accounts rules of BPA 1.0 (STD-KIND-029, -030,
STD-ISA-037), user accounts of the identity system, memory accounting, the `BILL` error area,
the governance layer `vendor`, and the example class of the `forge new` skeleton. CI runs the
scan.

**Procedure 2 — kernel changes of step 9.**

| Change | Part | Kind | Generic and standardised |

| `read_projection` selects its snapshot with every selector of convention 9 (`commit`, `tag`, `tx_time`), not only `time` | 9c (DEC-143) | conformance gap | yes: STD-CONF-005 convention 9 and the adapter row of `read_projection` already required it |
| `forge test` loads a seed file that needs a change set through an approved change set | 9a (DEC-141) | tool (Forge), not kernel | yes: the installation path of FR-PKG-031 |

Everything else the Buk uses was released as BPA 1.0 clauses before step 9: classes and
subclassing of genesis classes, ledgers with double entry, periods and close artefacts, the chart
of accounts, projections, query ports and L2 logic with `read.projection`. The Buk adds no
kernel code. **EXIT-2-04: passed by inspection.**
