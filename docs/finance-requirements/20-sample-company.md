# Sample Company — Northwind Components, Inc.

The sample company is fictitious. Its books are the common input of both implementations;
the expected results (`21-expected-results.md`) are the common acceptance oracle.

## 1. Company facts

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Legal name | Northwind Components, Inc. |
| Entity | Delaware C-corporation, headquarters Austin, Texas |
| Business | engineering services and industrial components, sold in the US and to Germany |
| Fiscal year | calendar year, 12 monthly periods |
| Functional currency | USD; foreign currency EUR |
| Time zone | America/Chicago |
| Banks | Lakeside National Bank: operating account (GL 1010), savings account (GL 1050); line of credit 50,000.00 at 7.20% |
| Books start | opening balances at 2025-12-31; first period 2026-01 |
| Income tax | federal 21% estimated monthly; Texas levies no corporate income tax (the Texas franchise tax is out of scope) |

## 2. Dataset files

All files are in `sample-company/`, UTF-8 CSV with a header row, amounts with two decimals
and dates `YYYY-MM-DD`. They are generated by `tools/gen_finance_expected.py`.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `chart-of-accounts.csv` | 36 accounts with type, normal balance and statement line |
| `customers.csv` | 4 customers (Texas, Oregon, Texas reseller, Germany) |
| `vendors.csv` | 8 vendors, 3 of them 1099-reportable |
| `tax-codes.csv` | 5 tax codes |
| `thresholds-1099.csv` | Form 1099-NEC/MISC thresholds by tax year |
| `fx-rates.csv` | EUR/USD rates used |
| `opening-balances.csv` | opening trial balance at 2025-12-31 |
| `open-receivables.csv`, `open-payables.csv` | open items at 2025-12-31 |
| `fixed-assets.csv` | 3 assets with accumulated depreciation at 2025-12-31 (FA-003 is acquired in January) |
| `transactions.csv` | all January and February 2026 documents as balanced lines, with posting date and recorded-on date |
| `bank-statement-2026-01.csv` | the January statement of the operating account with opening and closing balance and the expected match |

## 3. How the dataset is used

1. Load master data and opening balances (FIN-SCN-01).
2. Enter the January documents **through the user interface or the API of the
   implementation under test, in the order of `transactions.csv`**, not by loading
   `transactions.csv` as journal lines — except PAYROLL-2601, which is imported as a payroll
   file (FIN-DI-004). Invoices, bills, receipts, payments, credit memos, depreciation and
   revaluation are created by the functions that the requirements describe, so that the
   subledgers, tax, 1099 data and matches arise as they would in operation.
3. Generated amounts (tax, depreciation, revaluation, realized gain or loss, income tax
   provision) are **computed by the implementation** and compared with the expected results;
   they are never typed in.
4. After January, run the reports and compare them with FIN-EXP-03 … FIN-EXP-14. Close
   January.
5. Enter the February documents; JE-0005 is entered on (or with a simulated clock set to)
   2026-02-20. Compare with FIN-EXP-15 … FIN-EXP-17.

## 4. Rules fixed for the dataset

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Rounding | half away from zero, to the cent, per document and jurisdiction (tax), per asset and month (depreciation) |
| Approval | manual and imported journal entries above 10,000.00 need controller approval; bills above 10,000.00 need controller approval |
| Payment terms | net 30 from invoice date (City Power & Light: net 23) |
| Depreciation | full month in the month placed in service; no salvage; FA-002 double-declining by asset year |
| Revaluation | closing rate at month end, reversed on the first day of the next month |
| Sales tax | destination-based; TX-AUSTIN 8.25% (confirmed by the owner 2026-09-29); Oregon none; resale exempt with certificate; export none |
| 1099 | cash basis; 2026 threshold 2,000.00 from `thresholds-1099.csv` (confirmed by the owner 2026-09-29) |
| Cash and cash equivalents | accounts 1010 and 1050 |
| Income tax | 21% of the month's income before taxes, posted as JE-0004 |

## 5. Larger volumes

For performance tests (FIN-NF-001) each implementation uses a generated dataset of the
stated volumes that follows the same chart of accounts and rules. The generator and its
seed are shared so that both implementations are measured on the same data; its totals are
checked by recomputing the trial balance from the generated lines.
